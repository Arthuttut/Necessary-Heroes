**# 📄 Changelog – Necessary Heroes**

All notable changes to this addon will be documented in this file.

## Alpha [v1.0.2] Ben10, Rex Generator and NC: Core – 13/05/2025
### Added
- Animations for Omnitrix activation, dial rotation, and core tapping.
- Generator Rex: At the moment there is only the power called "nanite control" which is equivalent to Rex's powers.
- All ores and materials from Lucraft core: Hammer, Wire Cutter, Injection, Uranium, Uru, plates, wires among other things (However, some have redone textures)
- Recipes for processors(CPU, Advanced CPU and Vibranium CPU)
- Recipe for Reactor
- Hunger freeze ability(pauses the player's hunger completely this ability can be used on speedsters.. no, it's not cool to be hungry every 3 seconds and this will serve to control hunger)
- Clone ability(Undergoing testing! should not be used at all until my response about this ability. Ability bugs: After a while the wolf ends up dying and the Piglin does not follow the wolf at all and the Piglin is aggressive)

### Fixed
- Fixed alien fusion bug - After death while the watch was activated the selection menu remained activated and you could transform more than once even after already transforming, causing the fusion.

- Reactor Texture

- Fabrics crafts, now the craft is a dye, an iron plate and a leather
- CPU Texture
- Flux Capacitor Crafts and Circuits
